# Pricing Benchmark Notes

This packet uses external benchmark ranges as anchors, then translates them into a boutique fixed-bid posture.

## Anchors used
- Clutch web development agency rates
- Clutch web design rates
- Clutch UX/UI rates
- Upwork web developer marketplace ranges
- Arc freelance developer reference data

## Important note
These benchmarks vary widely because they blend geographies, team models, and delivery tiers. Your quote should reflect your actual speed, reuse advantage, operator overhead, and risk posture rather than the lowest available market rate.
