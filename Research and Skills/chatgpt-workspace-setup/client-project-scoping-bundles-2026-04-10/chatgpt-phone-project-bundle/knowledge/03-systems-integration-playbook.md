# Systems Integration Playbook

## Common website integration categories
- analytics and tagging
- CRM / email marketing
- payments and commerce
- digital fulfillment
- CMS and content storage
- SEO / sitemap / robots / llms discovery files
- blog publishing workflow
- AI-assisted authoring / approval
- affiliate catalog / payout / tracking
- support, telephony, or transactional email

## Integration analysis output shape
For each integration capture:
- business purpose
- confirmed requirement or assumption
- launch phase or later phase
- source of truth
- data created or exchanged
- owner / admin surface
- risk or compliance notes
