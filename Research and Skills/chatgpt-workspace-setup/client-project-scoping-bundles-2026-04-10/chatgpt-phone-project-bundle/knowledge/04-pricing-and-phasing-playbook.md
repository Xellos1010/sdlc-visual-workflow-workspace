# Pricing and Phasing Playbook

## Pricing method
Build pricing from:
- workstream
- effort band
- delivery speed
- technical risk
- revision count
- operator complexity
- future maintenance burden

## Always show two numbers
- standard market band
- recommended offer band

## Good phase structure
- Phase 0: discovery / style sprint / proof of direction
- Phase 1: launch-ready baseline
- Phase 2: CMS, dashboard, or content operations
- Phase 3+: advanced integrations, catalog, automation, or scale features

## Invoice rule
Each phase invoice should map to actual deliverables and exclude items that are still unknown.
