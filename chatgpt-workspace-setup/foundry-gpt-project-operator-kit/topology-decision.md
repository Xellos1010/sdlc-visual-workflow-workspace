# Topology Decision

## Recommended answer

Use **many specialist GPTs**, not one all-purpose GPT, once you are back on desktop.

Recommended desktop chain:
1. **Foundry Intake Orchestrator**
2. **Foundry Architecture Studio**
3. **Foundry Work-Order Factory**
4. **Foundry Verification & Release**
5. **Foundry Import Relay**

Optional:
- **Foundry Unified Operator** as a fallback for light use, demos, or when you want one entry point.

## Why many is better

A specialist chain is better when you want:
- strict role boundaries
- stable outputs by phase
- lower hallucination risk
- cleaner operator handoffs
- cheaper downstream execution because the planning artifacts are higher quality

## Why one GPT is still useful

A single GPT is useful when:
- you want minimal setup
- you are testing the process
- you want one place to triage before splitting into specialist threads

## Phone reality

You cannot build GPTs on mobile. Use the **Projects-only chain** from `phone-projects/` on the phone now, then optionally build the specialist GPTs later on desktop.
